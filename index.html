    Sources and files. IMF International Reserves and Foreign Currency Liquidity (IRFCL): line 56 gold volume and value, and total official reserve assets, for monetary authorities plus central government, fetched daily by a GitHub Actions job (scripts: gold, reserves; run logs: gold, reserves; data: gold, reserves, changes). Mine production: USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries (script, data, log) and Our World in Data (USGS and BGS figures, loaded by your browser). Funds: SPDR Gold Shares (loaded by your browser) and CFTC Commitments of Traders (weekly script, data, log). Dollar: US Treasury TIC (script, data, log) and IMF COFER (script, data, log). World Gold Council figures typed in by hand from Gold Demand Trends (data). Your trigger and notes: data/journal.json. The alert job's gauge is tested against the page's on every code change.

    Sources: IMF IRFCL, USGS, Our World in Data, SPDR Gold Shares, CFTC, US Treasury TIC, IMF COFER and the World Gold Council; scripts, data and run logs are listed under How the numbers are made. A personal project, not investment advice.
